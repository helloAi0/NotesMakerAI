What makes it different

Predict, run, explain

Pick a simulation, predict what changes, run it, and get an explanation of the gap. 177 simulations across physics, chemistry, maths and engineering.

Answers you can check

Every answer from your notes shows its source and page. Arithmetic is recomputed. When the model is unsure, it says so instead of guessing.

A model of you

A knowledge graph links your concepts, mistakes, simulations, notes and exam dates, then tells you what to work on next.

Mock exams that explain your marks

Timed papers from your weak topics, graded automatically, with a report on how you lose marks: rushing, concept gaps, repeats.

Find the exact wrong line

Photograph or type your working. Each line is recomputed and the first mistake is pointed out.

Teaches in your language

Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu, with the voices installed on your device.

For teachers and institutions

Course pack from a syllabus

Paste a syllabus, get units, flashcards, simulation assignments and a week-by-week exam plan, as one small file.

Class dashboard

See weak topics and students who need attention from their progress files. No student data leaves their devices.

A pilot kit that measures

The same test before and after, plus a survey, giving the average gain with a confidence interval and an honest verdict.
